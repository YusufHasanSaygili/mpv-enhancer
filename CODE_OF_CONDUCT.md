# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

MPV Enhancer is committed to a welcoming, safe, and productive community. We
expect everyone who participates in the project to treat other people with
respect, patience, and empathy, regardless of background, identity, experience,
or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Give constructive, specific feedback and assume good intent.
- Respect different experience levels and explain decisions clearly.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and its users.
- Protect other people's privacy and remove personal information from examples.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, personal
attacks, deliberate disruption, and publishing another person's private
information are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews,
and other spaces where someone represents the project. Report conduct concerns
privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub. Do not include secrets,
personal paths, or private media in a report.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation, or take
other proportionate action when behavior violates this policy. Reports will be
handled as privately and fairly as practical, with attention to the safety of
the person reporting the concern.
